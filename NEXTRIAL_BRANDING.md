# NexTrial - Brand Identity & Guidelines

## 🎯 Brand Overview

**NexTrial** is a next-generation AI-powered clinical trials research platform designed specifically for Indian physicians and medical professionals.

### Brand Name
**NexTrial**
- **Nex**: Next-generation, innovative, forward-thinking
- **Trial**: Clinical trials, research, evidence-based medicine
- **Combined**: The future of clinical trial management

### Tagline Options
1. **Primary**: "Next-Generation Clinical Trial Management"
2. **Alternative**: "AI-Powered Research for Modern Medicine"
3. **Short**: "The Future of Clinical Research"

## 🎨 Visual Identity

### Logo Concept
- Icon: Science flask or beaker with AI circuit pattern
- Typography: Modern, clean sans-serif
- Style: Professional yet approachable

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Primary Blue: `#1976d2` (Trust, professionalism, medical)
- Secondary Purple: `#9c27b0` (Innovation, AI, technology)

**Accent Colors:**
- Success Green: `#4caf50` (Completed, approved)
- Warning Orange: `#ff9800` (In progress, attention)
- Error Red: `#f44336` (Critical, urgent)
- Info Blue: `#2196f3` (Information, guidance)

**Gradients:**
- Primary Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #1976d2 0%, #9c27b0 100%)`
- Button Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%)`
- Hero Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(25, 118, 210, 0.1) 0%, rgba(156, 39, 176, 0.05) 100%)`

### Typography
- **Headings**: Inter, Roboto (Bold 700)
- **Body**: Inter, Roboto (Regular 400, Medium 500)
- **Monospace**: Roboto Mono (for IDs, codes)

## 📝 Brand Voice

### Tone
- **Professional**: Medical-grade accuracy and reliability
- **Innovative**: Cutting-edge AI and technology
- **Supportive**: Helpful, guiding, empowering
- **Clear**: No jargon, easy to understand

### Writing Style
- Use active voice
- Be concise and direct
- Focus on benefits, not just features
- Use medical terminology appropriately
- Address physicians as peers

## 🌟 Key Messages

### Primary Value Propositions
1. **AI-Powered Intelligence**: Leverage AI to accelerate research and protocol development
2. **Comprehensive Research**: Access 400,000+ trials and 35M+ research papers
3. **Time-Saving**: Reduce protocol development time by 70%
4. **Evidence-Based**: Build protocols backed by published research
5. **India-Focused**: Tailored for Indian medical professionals and CTRI compliance

### Target Audience
- **Primary**: Indian physicians and clinical researchers
- **Secondary**: Research coordinators, medical institutions
- **Tertiary**: Pharmaceutical companies, CROs

## 🚀 Product Features (Branded)

### Core Modules
1. **Research Hub** - Multi-source clinical trial and research paper search
2. **Protocol Builder** - AI-assisted ICH M11 compliant protocol creation
3. **AI Co-Pilot** - Real-time intelligent suggestions and guidance
4. **Document Generator** - Automated research summaries and reports
5. **My Documents** - Centralized document management
6. **Bookmarks** - Quick access to saved trials

### Feature Naming Convention
- Use clear, descriptive names
- Avoid overly technical jargon
- Emphasize benefits in descriptions
- Include AI/Smart/Intelligent where applicable
