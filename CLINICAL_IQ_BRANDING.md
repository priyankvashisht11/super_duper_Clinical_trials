# Clinical IQ - Brand Identity & Guidelines

## 🎯 Brand Overview

**Clinical IQ** is a next-generation AI-powered clinical trials research platform designed specifically for Indian physicians and medical professionals.

### Brand Name
**Clinical IQ**
- **Clinical**: Medical, professional, evidence-based
- **IQ**: Intelligence, smart, AI-powered, data-driven

### Brand Essence
- **Intelligent**: AI-powered insights and recommendations
- **Professional**: Built for medical professionals
- **Efficient**: Streamlines research workflows
- **Evidence-Based**: Data-driven decision making
- **Accessible**: User-friendly interface

## 🎨 Visual Identity

### Logo
- **Primary**: "Clinical IQ" text with brain/intelligence icon
- **Icon**: Brain, lightbulb, or neural network symbol
- **Style**: Modern, clean, professional

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
- **Primary Blue**: `#1976d2` - Trust, professionalism, medical
- **Secondary Purple**: `#9c27b0` - Innovation, AI, intelligence
- **Accent Teal**: `#00bcd4` - Fresh, modern, technology

#### Semantic Colors
- **Success Green**: `#4caf50` - Positive outcomes, completion
- **Warning Orange**: `#ff9800` - Alerts, attention needed
- **Error Red**: `#f44336` - Critical issues, errors
- **Info Blue**: `#2196f3` - Information, guidance

#### Neutral Colors
- **Dark Gray**: `#212121` - Primary text
- **Medium Gray**: `#757575` - Secondary text
- **Light Gray**: `#f5f5f5` - Backgrounds
- **White**: `#ffffff` - Cards, surfaces

### Typography

#### Headings
- **Font**: Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI'
- **Weights**: 700 (Bold), 600 (Semi-bold)
- **Style**: Clean, modern, professional

#### Body Text
- **Font**: Inter, system fonts
- **Weights**: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium)
- **Line Height**: 1.6-1.8 for readability

### Icons
- **Style**: Material Design Icons
- **Weight**: Regular (400)
- **Size**: 20px (small), 24px (medium), 32px (large)
